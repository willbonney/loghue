# Privacy Policy for Loghue

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Loghue colors Jenkins and CloudBees CI console logs in your browser. It does not collect log text, and it does not send log text to the developer or to any other service.

## What stays on the page

Loghue reads the console log that is already visible in the page so it can color errors, warnings, file paths, and similar lines. That text is not saved, and it is not transmitted.

## Settings

Loghue stores two kinds of settings:

- Whether highlighting is on or off
- The colors you choose

Those settings are stored by the browser. If you have browser sync turned on, Chrome or Firefox syncs them to your other signed-in browsers through your browser account. Loghue has no server of its own and does not receive those settings.

Removing the extension removes its access to those settings. You can also turn highlighting off from the toolbar icon, or reset the colors from the Colors page.

## What Loghue does not do

Loghue does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or cookies. It does not sell data. It does not share data with third parties. It does not use data for credit, lending, or purposes unrelated to coloring console logs.
